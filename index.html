Engineered for Sanskrit's Unique Structure

Vision & Restoration

दृष्टि-संस्कार

Manuscript degradation creates visual breaks that standard OCR mistakes for characters. Our vision models perform stain segmentation, background suppression, and character inpainting without hallucinating text.

  • Palm-leaf damage segmentation
  • Historical script recognition (Grantha, Śāradā, Tigalari)
  • Non-destructive digital restoration

Linguistic Intelligence

व्याकरण-विश्लेषण

Standard LLMs treat Sanskrit as unstructured tokens, causing wild hallucinations during sandhi splitting. Bhashita uses Ashtadhyayi rule-based constraints as a mandatory validation layer.

  • Pāṇinian-constrained sandhi-viccheda
  • Samāsa-vigraha (compound resolution)
  • Dhātu (verb root) & pratyaya derivation

Knowledge & Retrieval

भाष्य-परम्परा

A Sanskrit root text cannot be understood without its commentary chain. Bhashita indexes primary verses directly alongside historical bhāṣya, ṭīkā, and tippani lineages.

  • Sūtra-to-Bhāṣya knowledge graphs
  • Cross-textual citation tracking
  • Morphology-aware semantic search

Domain Applications

प्रयोग-क्षेत्र

Tailored analytical tooling built for multi-disciplinary scholarship—from extracting classical botanical formulations to assisting philological critical editions.

  • Ayurvedic botanical & formulation mapping
  • Interactive research assistant for philologists
  • Pedagogical grammar verification tools
